Training tip

The three-a-week rule

Hi there,

Most stuck players I meet work hard. Their chess time just goes to things that feel like training and don't move their rating: blitz, random videos, puzzle streaks.

In the Dojo we ask for "sweat work" three times a week. That means work that leaves you a little tired:

  • A long game
  • Going through that game on your own, before you turn on an engine
  • Hard exercises at your level

If you do one thing this week, play one game with at least 15 minutes on your clock. Then spend 20 minutes going through it alone, and mark the moment you felt unsure. That moment is your next lesson.
