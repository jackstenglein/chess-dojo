A training week that fits around a job

Hi there,

The most common reason people give me for not training properly is time. Fair enough. Plenty of Dojo members train around a full-time job.

Here's a week that takes about four hours:

- One long game (45 to 60 minutes), plus 20 minutes going through it
- One live class, or its recording when you can't make it
- Fifteen minutes of tactics on three days
- Your cohort's Discord channel when a question comes up

The plan tells you what to do each day, so you don't spend half your chess time deciding what to study.
